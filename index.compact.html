About NexusAI

We're building the AI layer that helps product teams ship faster without the chaos.

Our mission

Product teams spend too much time on status updates, handoffs, and rewriting the same specs. NexusAI automates the busywork so engineers and PMs can focus on building great products.

We believe the best teams win by reducing friction — not by adding more process. Our platform connects the tools you already use and uses AI to keep everyone aligned on what matters.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

NexusAI Inc. respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website and services.

Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email, company, and authentication details.
  • Usage data: product interactions and performance metrics.
  • Integration data: content you choose to sync from connected tools.
  • Billing data: processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the service
  • To process AI generations and workflow automation you request
  • To communicate about the product, security, and support
  • To detect abuse and ensure security

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with subprocessors necessary to run the service under appropriate contracts.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data. Contact privacy@nexusai.example.
