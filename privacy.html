Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

NexusAI Inc. (“NexusAI,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website and services.

  • Usage data: product interactions, feature usage, and performance metrics.
  • Integration data: content you choose to sync from connected tools (e.g. Slack, GitHub, Linear), subject to your configuration.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the service

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with subprocessors necessary to run the service (e.g. cloud hosting, payment processing, AI model providers), under appropriate contracts. Enterprise customers may request a current subprocessor list.

Security & retention

We use industry-standard security practices. Data is retained while your account is active and for a limited period afterward as required for legal or operational purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@nexusai.example to exercise these rights.
