Security

Enterprise-grade security is built into every layer of NexusAI.

✓ SOC 2 Type II
✓ Encryption in transit & at rest
✓ SSO / SAML
✓ Audit logs

Infrastructure

We run on major cloud providers with hardened configurations, network segmentation, and continuous monitoring. Production access is strictly controlled and logged.

Data protection

  • TLS 1.2+ for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Customer data isolation by workspace
  • Optional data residency for Enterprise plans

Access & identity

  • SSO (SAML / OIDC) and SCIM provisioning on Enterprise
  • Role-based access control
  • Mandatory MFA for internal systems
  • Short-lived credentials and least-privilege access

AI & third parties

We use leading AI model providers under contracts that prohibit training on your private data by default. You control which content is sent to AI features. Subprocessors are reviewed and listed for Enterprise customers on request.

Compliance & reporting

We maintain SOC 2 Type II. Enterprise customers can request our latest report and complete security questionnaires. To report a vulnerability, email security@nexusai.example.
